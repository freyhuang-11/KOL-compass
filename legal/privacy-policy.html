This Privacy Policy explains how [COMPANY] (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use Creator Outreach Tool (the “Service”), a creator-outreach tool for TikTok Shop sellers. By authorizing and using the Service, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information we collect

  • TikTok Shop authorized data. When you authorize your TikTok Shop via OAuth, we access data within the scopes you grant — shop information, products and categories, and TikTok Shop affiliate creator data (public creator profiles, metrics, and conversation/message data needed for outreach).
  • Account & usage data. Your account details, team members, settings, message templates, outreach records, sample and collaboration records you create in the Service.
  • Technical data. Standard logs (IP, timestamps, error logs) for security and reliability.

We do not collect data outside the scope you authorize, and we do not scrape data beyond what the TikTok Shop Open API provides under your authorization.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: sync your shop and products, present a creator library, send outreach (TikTok targeted invites, direct messages, emails) on your instruction, and track replies, samples and collaborations.
  • To translate your message copy into a creator’s local language and translate inbound replies, to support cross-language outreach.
  • To operate, secure, debug and improve the Service.

We use your TikTok Shop data solely to provide the Service to you. We do not sell your data and do not use it for unrelated purposes.

3. Sharing & third parties

We share data only as needed to run the Service:

  • TikTok Shop Open API — to read your authorized data and send outreach on your behalf, subject to TikTok’s terms and policies.
  • Translation provider — message text may be sent to a translation API to render it in the target language.
  • Email delivery — when you use the email channel, messages are sent through your configured email provider (SMTP).

We do not share your data with third parties for their own marketing.
