## 简要描述 Short description（≤200）

- EN：A creator-outreach SaaS for TikTok Shop cross-border sellers: sync shop products, filter creators by market and category, run bulk targeted invites / DMs / emails with auto-translation, and track conversations, samples and collaborations to boost reach and sales.

## 主要优势 Key advantages（3 条，标题≤50 / 描述≤950）

**① 一站式达人建联与履约 / End-to-end outreach & fulfillment**
- ZH：把"筛达人 → 发起建联 → 跟进会话 → 寄样 → 合作履约"做成一条闭环。商家授权店铺后即可同步商品、按市场/类目/粉丝/内容表现筛选达人，一键发起 TikTok 定向邀约、私信与邮件建联，并在同一界面跟进每个达人的沟通、寄样与合作进度，不再多工具来回切换。
- EN：A single closed loop from "find creators → start outreach → follow up → samples → collaboration". After authorizing their shop, sellers sync products, filter creators by market, category, followers and content performance, launch TikTok targeted invites, DMs and emails in one click, and track each creator's conversations, samples and deals in one place — no tool-switching.

**② 跨语言批量触达 / Cross-language outreach at scale**
- ZH：商家用中文写文案，系统按达人所在市场自动翻译成当地语言再发送；达人用当地语言回复时也能一键译回中文。配合消息模板与可配置的自动回复规则（关键词用中文维护、来信先翻译再匹配），让一个团队高效覆盖东南亚多市场达人。
- EN：Sellers write in Chinese; the system auto-translates to the creator's local language before sending, and translates replies back. With message templates and configurable auto-reply rules (keywords kept in Chinese, inbound messages translated before matching), a small team can efficiently cover creators across Southeast Asian markets.

**③ 数据驱动的达人筛选 / Data-driven creator selection**
- ZH：达人库展示粉丝量、内容表现、地区、类目等维度，并区分"有联系方式"与"基础数据"，帮商家在建联前判断达人质量、优先触达高潜力达人，提升回复率与履约率。
- EN：The creator library surfaces followers, content performance, region and category, and separates "with contact details" from "basic data", helping sellers judge creator quality before outreach and prioritize high-potential creators to improve reply and fulfillment rates.

## 详情描述 Detailed description（≥200，可贴富文本）
- ZH：
达人建联工具是一款面向 TikTok Shop 跨境商家的达人营销 SaaS。商家在 Seller Center 授权店铺后，系统通过 TikTok Shop Open API 同步店铺与商品，并提供按市场、类目、粉丝量、内容表现筛选的达人库。商家可对选中的达人一键发起 TikTok 定向邀约、私信和邮件建联，文案支持按达人所在市场自动翻译为当地语言。每一次建联都会归集到对应达人的会话里，方便跟进回复、安排寄样并转入合作管理。系统还提供消息模板与自动回复规则，帮助团队规模化、规范化地与达人沟通。我们严格在 TikTok Shop 授权范围内调用接口，不抓取授权之外的数据，所有店铺数据仅用于为该商家提供达人建联与履约服务。
- EN：
Creator Outreach Tool is a creator-marketing SaaS for TikTok Shop cross-border sellers. After a seller authorizes their shop in Seller Center, the app syncs the shop and products via the TikTok Shop Open API and provides a creator library filterable by market, category, followers and content performance. Sellers can launch TikTok targeted invitations, direct messages and emails to selected creators in one click, with copy auto-translated into each creator's local language. Every outreach is grouped into that creator's conversation, making it easy to follow up on replies, arrange samples and move into collaboration management. Message templates and auto-reply rules help teams communicate with creators at scale and consistently. We call the APIs strictly within the scope authorized by the seller, never collect data beyond that scope, and use shop data solely to provide creator-outreach and fulfillment services to that seller.
